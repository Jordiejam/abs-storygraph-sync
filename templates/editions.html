Editions

Sync and History Import only write to an edition you've confirmed here. Tick books and search StoryGraph for them, then check each suggestion and confirm it. With Auto-confirm Editions on in Settings, auto-sync also searches once for each book you start listening to and confirms only strong matches (the ABS tag, an ISBN/ASIN, or a runtime and narrator nothing else comes close to). Those wait under To review, and nothing is written to them until the next sync, so a wrong one can be undone first. Sync ABS tags confirms books already tagged storygraph:<id> in Audiobookshelf and tags the confirmed ones that aren't.
